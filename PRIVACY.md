# Privacy Policy — typad

typad is a local text editor. It does not create an account, does not include analytics, and does not send your files to a server.

## Data stored on this Mac

- Editor settings and the last session list are saved under Application Support (`~/Library/Application Support/typad/config.json`).
- Files you open or save stay wherever you put them.

## Data not collected

No name, email, location, contacts, usage analytics, or crash reports are collected by typad.
